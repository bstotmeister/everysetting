First release of Every Setting, for WoW Forever and retail from one download.

- **Every console variable in one window.** About 1,800 in Forever and 1,700 in retail, read from the client, with the game's other console commands and 70 settings that aren't CVars: the modified clicks, action bars 2 to 8, item quality colours, minimap tracking and more. Search by name, by the game's own description, or by value.
- **Real controls.** About 870 in Forever and 800 in retail get a slider, menu, checkbox or color swatch, with a range that comes from the game itself. Where the engine takes any number, the box beside the slider takes it too.
- **Each game its own.** Retail's rows take their names and controls from retail's own options, and its ranges from its own engine. The CVars only retail has, and the ones only Windows has, get controls of their own, and a menu greys out what your computer can't use.
- **Your values stay put.** Set again at every login, or at once with **Apply now**. Tick **Put back** and they're set again a second after something changes them.
- **The hidden settings players ask for.** Loot sparkles, HDR, nameplate distance, the spell queue window, the action camera, Advanced combat logging kept on, and the fifteen modified clicks the options menu never shows.
- **Undo, Redo and Reset.** Reset puts a setting back to how it was before you changed it. A setting you never move is never written.
- **Styles, profiles, presets, situations and locks.** Cinematic, Immersive, Competitive, Photo, Ultra+, True Max and Action, mixed part by part. Share a profile as a line of text. Each situation (in combat, mounted, in a city or inn, in an instance) has a preset of its own to start from.
- **Paste the commands from any guide.** Import takes `/console` lines and `SetCVar` macros as one change you can undo. Nothing pasted is ever run. Export gives yours back as `/console` lines.
- **The command for any setting.** A **/** button on every row gives the line that sets it, to type in chat or put in a macro. The 39 console commands tried in the game and found safe also have **Run**, which shows in chat what they print.
- **It tells you what happened.** A setting's tooltip says what last changed it and where the game saves it. The window shows your frame rate before and after a graphics change. **Not default** lists everything that isn't at the game's default.
- **Danger mode** for the settings the game locks: the ones it lets an addon change, through its own functions, and the rest, greyed out, so you can see them.
- **The game's own names** for the settings its menu has (314 in Forever, 258 in retail), in your game's language, a plain name for 79 more, and every word of Every Setting's own ready for translation.

Every CVar is listed in each game. The few settings one game lacks, such as loot sparkles and HDR on retail, are left out of its window.

Type `/every` to open the window. It's also under Options > AddOns, on a key you can bind, and in the minimap's addon list.
